What an account changes

Ordering and documentation adapt to institutional purchasing.

Wholesale pricing

Every catalogue size has a wholesale price. Once approved, you see it on every product page and it is applied at checkout - no quote request needed.

Minimum order

Wholesale prices apply from 10 vials of each size in the order.

Purchase orders

Add your purchase order number at checkout. It is recorded against the order for your accounts team.

Order history

Every order stays in your account, with the lot number of each vial you received.

Documentation access

Certificates for every lot you have received stay retrievable from the certificate library.

Saved orders

Save a standing order from the cart and add it back in one step when you restock.

How approval works

We verify that materials are going to a research setting. It is a short process, not a sales cycle.

01

Apply

One short form covering your organisation, research context and expected purchasing.

02

Review

We confirm the organisation and the research-use context. We may ask one or two follow-up questions by email.

03

Activate

Your account switches to wholesale pricing and we let you know by email.

Applications are reviewed individually. We do not publish a turnaround commitment, and we will tell you where the review stands if you ask.
