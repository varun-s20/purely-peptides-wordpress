Refund and returns policy

Returns window

Unopened material in its original, sealed packaging may be returned within 14 days of delivery. Contact us before returning anything so a return reference can be issued; returns sent without a reference cannot be matched to an order.

Return shipping is the customer's responsibility unless the return is the result of our error.

What cannot be returned

Opened vials cannot be returned. Once a seal is broken the chain of custody is broken and the material can no longer be attributed to the tested lot.

Materials stored outside the conditions stated on the product page, and materials whose lot label has been removed or defaced, cannot be returned.

Damaged, incorrect or short shipments

If material arrives damaged, does not match the order, or is short, tell us within five business days of delivery with photographs of the outer packaging, the vial and the lot label. We replace or refund at your choice and do not ask for damaged material to be returned.

How refunds are issued

Approved refunds are returned by ACH credit to the bank account used for the original payment. We do not issue refunds to a different account or by a different method.

Refunds are processed within five business days of the returned material being received and inspected, or of a damage claim being approved. Funds typically settle within a further three to five business days depending on the receiving bank.

Shipping charges are refunded only where the return results from our error.

Cancellations

An order can be cancelled without charge at any point before it is despatched. Once an ACH debit has been authorised at checkout it cannot be changed or corrected, but the order itself can still be cancelled and refunded by ACH credit if it has not yet shipped.

How to start a return

Email support@purelypeptideshub.com with your order number, the product and lot number, and what you would like us to do. We answer return requests within one business day.

Questions about this policy

Write to support@purelypeptideshub.com or use the contact form.
