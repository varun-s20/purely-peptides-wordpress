Storage & handling

Lyophilised material should be stored at −20 °C or below, desiccated and protected from light. Allow sealed vials to reach room temperature before opening. See our storage guidance.

Short ambient transit is not expected to affect sealed lyophilised material. Cold-chain shipping is available at checkout where a study requires it.

It depends on the peptide and the buffer. Aliquot and freeze immediately after reconstitution, and avoid repeated freeze-thaw cycles.
