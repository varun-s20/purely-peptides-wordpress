Chargeback policy

What to do if you do not recognise a payment, and how we handle disputed ACH debits.

Awaiting legal review. This page states our position on disputed ACH payments and should be reviewed by counsel alongside the terms of purchase.

Contact us first

If you do not recognise a charge, or something about your order is wrong, contact us before raising a dispute with your bank. Email support@purelypeptideshub.com with the order number and the amount. Almost every disputed payment we see is a billing-descriptor question or a delivery problem that we can resolve the same day.

Payments from this website appear on your bank statement as PURELY PEPTIDES HUB.

Authorised ACH debits

Each order is settled by a single ACH debit that you authorise at checkout by ticking the ACH debit authorisation and clicking Place Order. We retain a timestamped record of that authorisation, the account it applied to, the order it relates to, and the IP address it was given from.

An additional entry of less than $1 may appear alongside a first order. That is the account-validation micro-entry described in the terms of purchase, and it is reversed automatically.

How we respond to a dispute

Where a dispute is raised with your bank, we provide the payment processor with the order record, the ACH authorisation record, the research-use acknowledgement given at checkout, proof of despatch and carrier tracking, and the certificate of analysis issued for the lot supplied.

We do not contest disputes where material was not delivered, arrived damaged, or did not match the order. Those are refunds, and we would rather issue them directly.

Disputes we do contest

We contest disputes where the order was delivered and accepted as ordered, where the dispute is raised after the return window has closed, or where material has been opened and cannot be returned to inventory.

Repeated disputes raised without first contacting us may result in an account being closed and future orders declined.

Unauthorised activity

If you believe an ACH debit was taken from your account without authorisation, contact your bank immediately and tell us at the same time. We will place the account on hold, stop any further debits and cooperate fully with your bank's investigation.

Questions about this policy

Write to support@purelypeptideshub.com or use the contact form.
